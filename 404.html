Country quick guide

              
What the student visa looks like, country by country.

              
General notes only. Your consultant confirms what your own application needs.

            

            

              

                
                
                
                
                
                
              

              

                
🇨🇦Canada: study permit

                
You apply for a study permit, which often needs a provincial attestation letter (PAL).

                
Usually involves

                
  • Letter of acceptance from your school
  • Provincial attestation letter, in most cases
  • Proof of funds
  • Biometrics

                
Ask about Canada

              

              

                
🇺🇸USA: F-1 student visa

                
You apply for an F-1 visa with the Form I-20 from your school, then attend an embassy interview.

                
Usually involves

                
  • Form I-20 from your school
  • Proof of funds
  • Visa application form
  • Interview at the US embassy

                
Ask about the USA

              

              

                
🇬🇧UK: Student visa

                
You apply for a Student visa with a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from your university.

                
Usually involves

                
  • CAS from your university
  • Proof of funds
  • Valid passport
  • Biometrics

                
Ask about the UK

              

              

                
🇦🇺Australia: Student visa (subclass 500)

                
You apply with a Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) and must meet the Genuine Student requirement.

                
Usually involves

                
  • Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE)
  • Genuine Student requirement
  • Proof of funds
  • Health insurance

                
Ask about Australia

              

              

                
🇳🇿New Zealand: Fee Paying Student visa

                
Most international students apply for a Fee Paying Student visa for full-time study.

                
Usually involves

                
  • Offer of place from your school
  • Proof of funds
  • Valid passport
  • Health and character checks

                
Ask about New Zealand

              

              

                
🇪🇺Europe: national student visa

                
You apply for the national student visa of the country you choose; each has its own rules.

                
Usually involves

                
  • Admission letter from your university
  • Proof of funds
  • Health insurance
  • Appointment at that country's embassy

                
Ask about Europe

              

              

                
                
Requirements change — always confirm with us or the official embassy site.
